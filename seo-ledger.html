SEO Ledger

A focused WordPress SEO change-history concept that records what changed, who changed it, and why—without turning SEO maintenance into guesswork.

Best for: content teams and SEO maintenance workflows.

Built to solve: untraceable metadata, URL, and status changes.

Access: request access for the current release.

SEO changes deserve a proper record.

When a title, description, URL, status, or important page setting changes, teams often lose the reason and the timeline. SEO Ledger is designed to create a page-level history so work can be reviewed, explained, and improved with context.

It is useful for developers, marketers, and site owners who want more confidence when maintaining a growing website over time.

Why it is useful.

Page-level history

Record metadata, URL, status, notes, author, and timestamp changes per page.

Better handovers

Give the next person context instead of leaving SEO decisions inside messages and memory.

Safer maintenance

Make it easier to review a change before assuming it is the cause of a visibility issue.

Useful audit trail

Keep a practical record for ongoing optimisation and content governance.
