Depakar Article Grid

A standalone WordPress plugin that turns any page into a clean, interactive article grid—without depending on a specific page template or theme.

Best for: blog, news, resource, and knowledge-base pages.

Built to solve: hard-to-browse archives and static article listings.

Access: available on WordPress.org.

Let visitors find the article they actually need.

When a site has more than a few posts, a long archive becomes frustrating. Depakar Article Grid gives visitors useful controls to search, sort, filter by category, year, or tag, and load more articles without a page reload.

It is useful for teams that publish regularly but do not want a custom-built archive every time their content grows. Because it is a standalone plugin, it can be added to any theme while labels remain manageable from wp-admin.

Why it is useful.

Better content discovery

Search, sorting, and filters reduce the time it takes to reach relevant articles.

Works with your theme

Use it as a plugin rather than rebuilding a page template or changing your existing theme.

Cleaner reading journeys

Readers can browse a growing content library without repeatedly loading separate archive pages.

Admin-controlled labels

Control visitor-facing labels from wp-admin so the interface matches your site language.

Ready to improve article discovery?

Install it from WordPress.org for immediate use. If you need help deciding whether it fits your content structure, request access and setup guidance directly.
