FormMailbox

A clean WordPress form plugin designed around a submission inbox, email fallback, templates, security checks, and a simple admin experience.

What it helps with

FormMailbox is designed for websites that need a reliable contact, enquiry, quote, or feedback form without an unnecessarily complicated workflow. Entries are stored in WordPress and can also be delivered by email.

Core capabilities

  • Contact, quote, course-enquiry, and feedback templates
  • Submission inbox and per-form shortcodes
  • Admin notification and optional user confirmation
  • Honeypot, timing check, maths question, and reCAPTCHA options
