FormMailbox

An inbox-first WordPress form plugin for teams that want reliable enquiries, stored submissions, and email delivery—without a complicated form workflow.

Best for: contact, quote, course enquiry, and feedback forms.

Built to solve: missed submissions and cluttered form setups.

Access: request access for the current release.

Forms should not make you wonder if a lead was missed.

FormMailbox is built around a simple idea: submissions should live safely inside WordPress and be sent by email as a useful fallback. That gives a site owner an inbox to review, while keeping the visitor experience straightforward.

It is useful for websites that need a practical form system without turning WordPress into an overcomplicated tool stack.

Why it is useful.

Inbox-first submissions

Keep form entries in WordPress so an email delivery issue does not hide an important enquiry.

Start faster

Use contact, quote, course-enquiry, and feedback templates rather than rebuilding common forms.

Protect form quality

Use honeypot, timing checks, maths questions, and reCAPTCHA options to reduce spam.

Keep users informed

Configure admin notifications and optional confirmation messages for a clearer form process.
