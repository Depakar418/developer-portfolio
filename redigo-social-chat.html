Redigo Social Chat

A simple WordPress social-chat plugin designed to give visitors a quick, direct route to start a conversation with a business.

Best for: service businesses and lead-focused websites.

Built to solve: friction between interest and first contact.

Access: request access for the current release.

Make it easier for a visitor to take the next step.

A visitor can be ready to talk but still leave if getting in touch feels slow or unclear. Redigo Social Chat is designed as a focused entry point from a WordPress website into a direct social conversation.

It is useful for teams that want a simple contact option alongside forms—especially when a fast response can turn interest into an actual conversation.

Why it is useful.

Lower contact friction

Give visitors a familiar, direct route to start a conversation.

Useful alongside forms

Provide a faster option for people who do not want to complete a longer enquiry form.

Simple business use

Keep the interaction focused instead of adding an overbuilt chat workflow.

Clear conversion path

Place a purposeful contact action at the point where visitors are ready to ask.
