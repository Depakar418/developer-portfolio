Keeping AMP and mobile experiences useful

A lighter mobile page must still deliver the information and actions a visitor expects.

Keep the essential journey intact

Mobile users still need clear headings, key information, navigation, forms, validation messages, and meaningful confirmation. Removing these to make a page lighter can make it less useful even when its score improves.

Use the same content priorities

Important information should not be hidden behind desktop-only elements. Make the main message, proof, FAQs, and next step easy to reach without excessive scrolling or confusing interaction patterns.

Test forms and tracking

AMP or lightweight pages need separate validation for form submission, confirmation states, analytics events, and legal links. Test success and error states as carefully as the visual layout.

Review the page as a visitor

Check it on a real small screen. The page should load, explain its purpose, support the expected action, and return a clear result without asking the visitor to switch to another version.
