Clear learning journeys for important content.

Compliance content can be complex, but the digital experience should not be. eLearnPOSH is approached as a focused learning journey where page structure, responsive behaviour, and clear calls to action work together.

My work supports the development and ongoing improvement of WordPress pages and learning-facing content, with attention to accessibility, content hierarchy, performance, and technical quality.

What I worked on.

Responsive page creation

Developing clear page layouts for desktop, tablet, and mobile learning journeys.

Content hierarchy

Using meaningful H1, H2, and H3 structure so important information remains easy to scan.

Technical SEO

Supporting technical hygiene and content patterns that help key pages remain discoverable.

Performance awareness

Making practical implementation choices with page speed and user experience in mind.

Analytics support

Supporting measurement foundations so content and lead journeys can be reviewed over time.

Maintenance

Testing, production checks, and iterative updates as course and business needs develop.

Designed for sustained improvement.

Learning-first UX

Structure helps users focus on what they need to learn, instead of working around the interface.

Search & AI readiness

Clear topic architecture and semantic content support SEO, AEO, and GEO-oriented improvement work.

Extensible delivery

Maintainable WordPress implementation supports new pages, content formats, integrations, and reporting needs.

Project questions.

What is the main focus of this work?

Creating reliable, responsive WordPress experiences for compliance learning content and the journeys around it.

How is content quality maintained?

By keeping page hierarchy clear, testing responsive behaviour, and reviewing implementation details during ongoing updates.

Can the platform grow with new content?

Yes. Reusable page patterns and maintainable content structure make it easier to support future courses, campaigns, and integrations.
